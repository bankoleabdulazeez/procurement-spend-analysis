# Procurement Insights

## Executive Summary

The procurement dataset contains 500 transactions with total spend of **€1,240,580.53**.

The analysis shows that procurement spend is highly concentrated in a small number of categories, suppliers, and items. This creates clear opportunities for strategic sourcing, supplier negotiation, and spend prioritization.

## 1. Category Concentration

Electronics is the largest category at **€697,805.23**, representing **56.25%** of total procurement spend.

Software is the second-largest category at **€336,018.00**.

Together, Electronics and Software account for approximately **83.33%** of total procurement spend.

### Recommendation

Procurement teams should prioritize strategic sourcing and negotiation in Electronics and Software because improvements in these categories can have a much larger financial impact than savings in low-spend categories.

## 2. Supplier Concentration

TechMart Inc. is the largest supplier with **€328,761.73** in spend, representing **26.50%** of total spend.

QuickDeliver Ltd. is second with **€285,353.59**, representing **23.00%**.

Together, the two largest suppliers account for **49.50%** of total procurement spend.

### Recommendation

Review supplier concentration and dependency. Benchmark prices, negotiate volume discounts, and maintain alternative suppliers where appropriate.

## 3. High-Value Items

The largest individual spending items are:

1. Laptop — €472,284.81
2. Annual Software License — €336,018.00
3. Monitor — €130,072.59
4. Printer — €95,447.83
5. Desk Chair — €91,857.82

Laptops alone account for **38.07%** of total spend.

### Recommendation

Treat laptops and software licenses as strategic purchasing categories. They should receive more sourcing attention than low-value office supplies.

## 4. ABC Analysis

The ABC analysis classifies items according to cumulative spend:

- **A:** €938,375.40 — 75.64%
- **B:** €187,305.65 — 15.10%
- **C:** €114,899.48 — 9.26%

A relatively small group of high-value items therefore represents the majority of procurement spend.

### Recommendation

Use tighter controls, supplier negotiations, and regular reviews for A-class items. C-class items can generally use simpler purchasing processes.

## 5. Electronics Savings Opportunity

Illustrative savings scenarios for Electronics:

| Scenario | Potential Savings |
|---|---:|
| 3% | €20,934.16 |
| 5% | €34,890.26 |
| 10% | €69,780.52 |

These figures represent hypothetical savings if the corresponding percentage reduction were achieved. They are not realized savings.

### Recommendation

Electronics should be a priority for competitive bidding, volume discounts, contract renegotiation, and supplier benchmarking.

## Overall Procurement Priorities

1. **Electronics** — largest category and biggest immediate savings opportunity.
2. **Laptops** — highest individual item spend.
3. **TechMart Inc.** — highest supplier spend and major Electronics exposure.
4. **Software licenses** — second-largest item/category spending area.
5. **Supplier concentration** — top two suppliers represent nearly half of total spend.
